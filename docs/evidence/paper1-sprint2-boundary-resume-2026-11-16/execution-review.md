## Segment and completion graph

The frozen campaign counts every started attempt, including a killed attempt,
toward three total segments. Each allocation is limited to 48 hours. Valid
terminal resolved or scientific-failure records are verified and skipped by
later segments, rather than fitted again. A fourth started attempt is refused.
An unfinished task after segment three remains an execution/missing failure.

The fit branch is three native `afterany` arrays, followed by the probe-plan
producer, then the exact comparison workers, then a receipt-reconciliation
and notification job. Two historical recovery branches and the interrupted
twin's three segments/controller evidence and final comparison must also be
represented in the aggregate completion status. Completion dependencies must
use `afterany` so a failed worker does not suppress the failure notification.
The twin comparison must wait for both its final attempt and the corresponding
uninterrupted replay result.

The provided segment helper registers all three held fit arrays before
release, but releases them before a downstream graph has been registered.
Launch orchestration should register the complete dependent graph while held,
or finish downstream registration immediately and verify it before leaving the
run. Preserve accepted job IDs on partial submission failure.

The existing exact probe engine checkpoints each required location. Its plan
does not grant an unbounded retry budget. Record the actual probe allocation
limit and any separately bounded retry allowance before execution; missing
or failed comparisons remain explicit. A probe-plan producer may have fewer
than 24 runnable tasks when a required fit lacks a terminal receipt. That
must remain visible in the plan and final summary, not become a smaller
successful comparison denominator.

The prior operational finalizer caught reconciliation command errors but then
copied expected output files unconditionally under `set -e`. A reconciliation
that fails before writing its ledger can therefore prevent aggregate-index
generation. The new operational finalizer must guard optional copies and reads,
always attempt to write a completion index with unavailable/error statuses,
and return nonzero when requirements are unmet. Preserve separate branch
statuses for recovery and twin evidence. A completion job's scheduler success
or an email is not a scientific acceptance verdict. The existing summary's
`qualification_pass = false` and required subsequent review remain appropriate.
