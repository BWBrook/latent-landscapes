Of the 168 old resolved dense/lean pairs, nine shared at least five original locations in each spatial regime. The other 159 could never pass the current reducer, even with a complete paired audit. **Probe reuse would change the comparison.** At an existing coordinate the registered audit tests fresh eligibility, the free maximum, set membership, score drift and applicable nuisance contacts. It reports the original criterion and can still fail on fresh evidence. In the 24 archived one-sided replays, all 3,785 probes at the candidate’s own nodes passed those checks. Nevertheless, 2,255 produced a different best nuisance-parameter vector. The subsequent search uses preceding probe results as starts. Skipping these calls would change the evidence and can change results at the missing coordinates. ## Evaluator cost before training

All 18 archived order-192 banks load with `max_x=5`. Widening to 40 cleared their truncation check. On ten sites from an archived 260-site dataset at h=30, η=−2.5, the narrow and wide evaluations took median **83.82 and 1.12 ms** over seven trials. Log values differed by at most 2.84×10⁻¹⁴. The series-tail test bounds truncation. At x=39.2 and h=1.2 the third derivatives differed by 4.45×10⁻⁹ in floating-point arithmetic. No fit change was established by that stress check. [Series and raw evaluators](../../../src/latent_landscapes/differentiable_likelihood.py)

The S11 start uses only the site log values. Passing batched values to the unchanged start selection chose the same indices at all 260 sites. A complete 13-start audit of one archived fitted location took **2.16 s with scalar evaluation and 0.84 s with batching**. Every returned scientific field matched. This case had zero raw-fallback cells on the S11 grid. The historical 44.5% runtime share covers the entire latent-audit stage. Benchmark whole fits on ordinary and costly development cases before changing production or estimating training cost. The proposed per-site vectorisation has no measured implementation here. ## Claims and calibration

- **Field design.** Arm C adds 140 prey sites outside the 120-site predator network. The estimator integrates each site’s arrivals against its own camera history. Cameras elsewhere give those 140 sites no direct update to their arrival prior. Shared fitted effects still couple the prey observations. Report A versus C as the equal-prey-visit comparison under this network and procedure. The complete-data reference reveals true losses, arrivals and hidden pre-period events, so it cannot isolate the value of network conditioning. A sensitivity analysis that conditions on the same observed cameras in both arms could address that question if independent banks give adequate effective sample sizes. [Design and oracle](../../paper1-integrated-tranche-protocol-2026-10-18.md)
- **Training.** Freeze the scope of claims and the map-population upper bound before training. Each family has 108 stationary strata and 648 stratum–truth checks for intervals. Requiring every stratum to pass can sharply reduce the chance of qualification. At a fixed cutoff a valid bound on the true worst stratum suffices for the all-strata decision. The checks reuse maps and histories, so multiplying independent-cell pass probabilities gives no campaign probability. Hierarchical pooling needs prospective coverage checks under exceptional-map and heterogeneous scenarios. Prespecified narrower scopes can inform interpretation if the full result stays visible. [Training target and alternatives](../paper1-sprint2-pilot-2026-11-08/training-feasibility-review.md)
- **Theory and ecology.** Composite-likelihood boundary theory depends on the profiled nuisance parameters and regularity assumptions. It gives no fixed grid-step difference between declaration and interval cutoffs. Paper 1 conditions on a specified spread law and camera detection mean and models absorbing local loss. Its truth-assisted attenuation supports a role for risk-set selection. The remaining corridor/window-low B failure establishes exhaustion of the outer budget at a candidate above the eligible envelope. An ecological ridge remains a hypothesis.
