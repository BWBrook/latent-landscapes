# Goal 043: Arc 2A Active-Path Consolidation

## Programme role

This is the bounded consolidation tranche required after Roadmap Arc 2A. It
changes no science. It reduces the accepted predator-only workflow to the
smallest coherent path that can reproduce the 32-truth campaign and Goal 042
synthesis.

## Lean product promise

For Barry, collaborators, and later Arc 2 work, the retained Arc 2A code must:

1. generate the frozen `S01-S32` truth design and no-resistance diagnostic;
2. run either 16-truth campaign block with the accepted simulation and
   inference engine; and
3. synthesize the two compact campaign bundles with the genuinely prospective
   prediction table into the accepted Arc 2A review evidence.

Historical goal-by-goal report generators are not part of the supported
product. Git and the completed goal records preserve that development history.

## Measured baseline

Before consolidation, Goals 038-042 occupy:

- five numbered source modules: `4,451` lines;
- five numbered runners: `253` lines;
- five test files: `587` lines and 13 tests; and
- a final synthesis that imports private helpers from Goal 040 and a provenance
  constant from Goal 041.

The full repository test suite collected 274 tests and completed in about 30
seconds on the Mac mini. Runtime is not the principal problem; comprehension,
duplicated artifact machinery, and backward dependencies are.

## Protected scientific invariants

- scrambled Sobol seed `20260829` and exact truth coordinates `S01-S32`;
- accepted factor domains, observation design, common-random-number roots,
  calibration policy, likelihood, and neutral comparator;
- exact tracked Goal 041 prospective-prediction table and its source binding;
- fixed Goal 040 model forms, response scales, thresholds, and predictor
  scaling used by Goal 042;
- paired bootstrap seed `20260830` and validation-history resampling unit;
- Goal 042 outcome, prospective scores, point-error maxima, `S10/S19`
  resistance-width flags, `S20` coverage flag, 32-of-32 neutral wins, and
  combined descriptive model diagnostics; and
- the plain-language synthesis and candidate figure semantics.

## Consolidation contract

1. Replace the numbered Goal 038-042 active modules with three scientific
   components named for their role: Arc 2A design, campaign, and synthesis.
2. Retain one campaign runner supporting the pilot or prospective block and
   one synthesis runner. Delete the five superseded goal-specific runners.
3. Move only the truth-design logic needed by the accepted campaign. Delete
   Goal 038's completed ecological-translation and review-pack machinery.
4. Keep the Goal 039 simulation and inference engine as the canonical campaign
   kernel, renamed and minimally adapted rather than rewritten.
5. Make the synthesis self-contained. It must load both standard campaign
   bundles and calculate prospective scores directly from the tracked frozen
   prediction table before fitting the combined descriptive surfaces.
6. Delete Goal 040 and Goal 041 interim surface/report machinery. Their accepted
   evidence remains in Goal records, Git history, and the frozen prediction
   table.
7. Collapse the five test files into a small suite covering distinct risks:
   exact design, paired campaign semantics, frozen prospective evidence,
   scientific synthesis invariants, and exact final bundle output.
8. Add no compatibility imports, deprecated aliases, migration scripts,
   deletion ledger, or archive copies.

## Objective acceptance

- no production imports or runners named `goal038` through `goal042` remain;
- the supported Arc 2A flow is legible as design -> campaign -> synthesis;
- active Arc 2A Python line count falls by at least 35%;
- the final Goal 042 evidence reproduces every protected numerical and
  categorical invariant, with floating-point comparisons at `1e-12`;
- focused tests and the full repository quality/build gate pass once; and
- generated evidence and build outputs remain ignored.

## Success and stop rules

Perform one serious consolidation pass and one material repair pass if needed.
Stop when the active flow and protected evidence pass. Do not pursue formatting
polish, whole-repository cleanup, further abstraction, faster simulation, or
unrelated test pruning.

## Authority and boundaries

Barry approved Goal 043 on 31 August 2026. Exact-path deletion, renaming,
implementation, ignored artifact regeneration, branch commit, and review-
branch publication are authorized. Canonical `main` publication remains a
later human decision.

Do not run new production simulation, alter accepted evidence, change a

## Observed result

Goal 043 completed the authorized consolidation without new simulation or a
scientific change.

- **Retained:** the exact `S01-S32` Sobol design and diagnostic truth, the
  accepted campaign kernel, the tracked frozen prospective predictions, the
  four fixed descriptive model forms, and the compact Arc 2A synthesis.
- **Simplified:** the supported flow is now
  `arc2a_design -> arc2a_campaign -> arc2a_synthesis`, exposed by one campaign
  runner and one synthesis runner.
- **Deleted:** the numbered Goal 038-042 production modules and runners, their
  interim report machinery, and tests that protected no distinct active risk.
  Git and the completed goal records remain the historical record.
- **Deferred:** Arc 2B, broader repository cleanup, and any further figure or
  prose polish.

The active Arc 2A Python surface fell from `5,291` to `2,698` lines (`49.0%`),
and its tests fell from 13 to five. The canonical synthesis now takes only the
pilot campaign bundle, prospective campaign bundle, and tracked frozen
prediction table. It reconstructs the prospective evidence itself rather than
depending on the deleted interim surface and validation modules.

Regeneration reproduced the accepted Goal 042 outcome and every protected
numerical and categorical result. The five compact scientific CSVs were byte
identical; the protected summary fields matched exactly; and the synthesis
prose retained the accepted interpretation while updating its workflow
language. Generated evidence and build outputs remained ignored.

The proportional release gate passed: five focused Arc 2A tests and all 265
repository tests passed; Ruff formatting and lint, Mypy, `git diff --check`,
and package build also passed. The three warnings were pre-existing Matplotlib
deprecation notices in Goal 006 and do not affect this tranche.
