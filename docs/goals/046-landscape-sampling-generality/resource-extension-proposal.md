## Why the existing ceiling prevents the next comparison

The three completed K65536 campaigns took 22863–23025 seconds (6.35–6.40 hours).
For barrier 2026090811, bank generation took 7772 seconds, integration 12442,
and calibration plus evaluation 2652. A simple bank-linear extrapolation to
K131072 gives about **12 hours per map** for that campaign alone; it is a
planning estimate, not a runtime guarantee. Approximately 5 hours 35 minutes
remain at this decision. Even the completed K65536 campaign duration exceeds
that remainder.

All nine maps would then require the selected-K65536 full replay and separate
half-grid checks. The original selected-K16384 reviews took 1.36–1.65 hours;
a linear planning allowance at K65536 is roughly **5.5–6.6 hours**. Allow about
18–20 hours for the new campaign and reviews, plus transfer, verification and
scientific synthesis/review if every map qualifies. Later map completions and
uncertain scaling require contingency. Launching the next uniform pair inside
the existing window cannot plausibly complete the required qualification.
