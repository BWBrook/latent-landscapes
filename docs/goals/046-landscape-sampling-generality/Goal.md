## Question and completion condition

Does access-constrained sampling limit prey-effect recovery, and can spatially
balanced sampling improve recovery at the same survey effort across contrasting
landscapes?

Complete an interpretable matched comparison across connected,
corridor-dependent and barrier-dominated landscapes, with several independent
map realizations per archetype. Separate variation among maps from process and
observation variation within maps. Report paired sampling-design changes with
uncertainty, absolute recovery, common-target prediction and explanatory
landscape/event descriptors. Classify results as robust across the tested maps,
landscape-dependent, sampling-dependent or unresolved. A null benefit or weak
recovery is a valid completion outcome. Review independently and perform only
material consolidation of the active path.

## Settled design before the pilot

Use three independently seeded matched triplets, giving nine production maps.
Within a triplet the categorical inventory, source geometry and broad seed
identity are matched across archetypes; different triplets are independent.
Production map seeds are 2026090811, 2026090812 and 2026090813. The sacrificial
pilot seed is 2026090801 and is excluded from production. The new master seed
is 2026090846; sampling and campaign branches are separate, with independent
campaign streams between maps and common random numbers across the three effect
truths within a map. This does not make the effect cases independent replicates.

Every map retains the 72 by 84 grid, three western source strips (81 cells),
823 water cells, 3449 matrix cells, 835 medium-quality cells, 682 high-quality
patch cells and 178 corridor cells. The accepted categorical conductances and
edge-cost equation are unchanged. Connectivity and geometry change; costs and
arrival times are not renormalized to make the waves match across maps.
All sampled cells must be source-reachable; disconnected islands are outside
this first landscape family. Generator details and structural diagnostics are
recorded in the pilot review before the production construction is frozen.

Keep the existing six-road/six-transect lattice, fixed independently of the
landscapes. Reserve two holdout clusters per Euclidean source-distance stratum
from the full eligible domain using seeded randomized maximin selection.
Then select six training clusters per stratum: randomized eligible-centre
selection on the access lattice, or randomized greedy maximin over the full
eligible domain. All selected centres are at least eight cells apart within
a design, including the common holdout buffer. Maximin uses coordinates only;
no realised arrivals, prey outcomes or fitted quantities select locations.

This is a comparison of two sampling strategies, combining eligibility and
placement. It does not isolate a road-only causal effect. Equal effort means
equal site-visits, not equal travel cost. Prediction targets the same finite
40 held-out sites in each map, with the same observed histories; it is not an
unweighted estimate over every landscape cell or a comparison of different
holdout targets. The fixed access lattice limits generality across access
networks and is not itself a replicated factor.

Use 250 independent calibration and 250 evaluation histories in each map,
with local rank 239 for each effect cutoff, separately by effect, sampling
design and analysis lane. The three map realizations, rather than the 250
histories or 120 sites, are the landscape replication. The small pilot uses
reduced counts solely for implementation and numerical diagnosis.

The five declared sampling contrasts remain separate outcomes. Report every
map and the equally weighted map mean within each archetype, separately by
prey effect and speed-information lane. Primary bootstrap intervals condition
on these fixed maps: independently resample complete calibration and evaluation
indices within each map, with each draw shared across designs, effects and
lanes, and recalculate local cutoffs. Use 1000 resamples. Display map ranges and
leave-one-map-out sensitivity alongside those intervals. These intervals do
not quantify uncertainty about unobserved landscapes; three maps per archetype
support a bounded generality test, not population-wide archetype inference.
No site bootstrap, pooled calibration or automatic map enrichment is permitted.

Report effect accuracy, calibrated effect support width/topology/coverage,
null exclusion or false exclusion, and common-target conditional prey prediction.
Rho point accuracy supplies context for the estimated-speed lane. Calibrated
rho intervals are not an endpoint of this sampling-design question; do not
claim their calibration or apply an unreported rho-tail cutoff as a surrogate
scientific criterion.

## Prospective numerical review limits

The following limits are recorded before new-map recovery or numerical-comparison
outcomes are inspected. Use nested banks and identical simulated histories.
Centre each compared profile at its own maximum. LR means twice the loss in
log likelihood; the maximum-gain criterion below is deliberately in log-likelihood
units and also states its LR equivalent.

For the first two calibration and first two evaluation histories, all three
effects and both designs:

- effect MLE movement at most one H181 cell (0.05 hazard-multiplier units);
- rho MLE movement at most two G201 cells (absolute log change 0.027726,
  allowing numerical rounding);
- effect and rho profile LR movement at most 0.5 over the union where either
  profile has LR <= 10, and direct generating-value LR movement at most 0.5;
- local half-grid maximum gain at most 0.25 log-likelihood units, equivalent
  to 0.5 LR units, with the effect and rho refinements examined separately.

The reduced execution pilot cannot establish full-ensemble cutoff, coverage
or exclusion stability. Before accepting a map's scientific evidence, compare
the reported full-ensemble quantities at the selected and doubled bank:

- effect cutoff movement <= 0.5 LR;
- unchanged effect-support component and boundary classifications, with
  endpoints moving at most one H181 cell;
- each design/lane effect coverage and exclusion count changes by at most one
  evaluation history, and each balanced-minus-access coverage/exclusion
  contrast changes by at most 1/250;
- numerical movement in the sampling contrast <= 0.005 for mean absolute log
  error, <= 0.01 for mean log width, and <= 0.0001 nats per holdout site for
  conditional prey score.

Numerical failures prompt bounded diagnosis; they do not authorize dropping a
criterion or enlarging the scientific design. Use pilot timing to choose the
initial bank and estimate the full selected/doubled-bank campaign cost before
launch. If accepted effect cutoffs extend beyond the checked LR range, extend
the numerical review to the reported range before scientific acceptance.
The comparisons remain conditional nested-bank evidence, not proof of exact
integration or independent Monte Carlo replication.
