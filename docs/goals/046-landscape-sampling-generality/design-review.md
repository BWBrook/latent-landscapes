# Arc 3 design-stage landscape review

Status: implementation review for the bounded design tranche. This records the
constructed maps and feasibility evidence; it does not approve production
recovery or make a biological claim.

The reproducible gallery and structural record are in
`artifacts/arc3_design/`:

- `production_landscape_gallery.png` and `.svg`: the nine production maps,
  three map seeds by three archetypes;
- `pilot_sampling_gallery.png` and `.svg`: access-constrained and balanced
  designs for the three pilot archetypes; and
- `structural_descriptors_and_checks.json`: descriptors, map checks, sampling
  seeds and sampling checks for all twelve maps.

The gallery was generated by `scripts/build_arc3_gallery.py`. It uses the
production seeds `2026090811`, `2026090812` and `2026090813`, the sacrificial
pilot seed `2026090801`, and sampling seeds generated as
`SeedSequence([MASTER_SEED, map_seed, 1]).generate_state(1)[0]`. No recovery
outcomes, fitted quantities or campaign results are read by this workflow.

## Construction

Every map uses the existing 72 by 84 `goal001.landscape.Landscape` schema and
the same three western source strips: 81 source cells in rows 6--13, 31--39
and 56--65, columns 0--2. The categorical inventory is exact on every map:

| class | cells |
| --- | ---: |
| water | 823 |
| low quality matrix | 3449 |
| medium quality | 835 |
| high quality patch | 682 |
| corridor | 178 |
| source zone | 81 |

The connected and corridor maps share the seeded water geometry for a given
map seed. Twelve jittered compact ellipse or scored-disk footprints are ranked
to select exactly 823 water cells. The corridor skeleton is protected during
this construction. It consists of two horizontal routes from column 3 to 60,
with rows jittered within the approved 10 +/- 3 and 60 +/- 3 envelopes, joined
by a vertical route at column 40 +/- 4. The implementation uses a two-cell
jitter so the route union remains feasible; its union is 161--169 cells, and
nearest eligible cells fill the corridor inventory to 178 without dropping
internal skeleton cells.

The connected archetype assigns its 178 corridor labels as spatially separated
cells. Its high and medium classes are selected from broad, redundant
east-west band scores. The corridor archetype assigns the protected skeleton
and its nearest cells to the corridor class, then selects high-quality cells
around six jittered patch cores and medium-quality cells from their broader
halos. The barrier archetype uses the connected class-layout rule but places
water in three width-four, near-spanning vertical bands around columns
26, 46 and 66 with deterministic one-cell column jitter. Each band has a
protected six-row passable gap near rows 12, 36 and 60 with deterministic
jitter. Remaining water cells are selected from compact filler scores.

The gap protection is a narrow construction correction made during this
review: filler selection now excludes all declared gap cells. Thus the six-row
gaps are part of the barrier definition rather than an accidental result of
the final water count.

All three generators verify that the exact inventory holds, source geometry is
unchanged, every passable cell is source-reachable, and the passable rook graph
has one component. When the initial compact water ranking would disconnect the
passable graph, the generator deterministically opens shortest paths through
water between passable components and refills the opened cells from the
lowest-scoring eligible candidates while retaining one component. This is a
structural feasibility repair within the prescribed map construction; it does
not retry seeds, inspect recovery, or select maps on outcomes. A failure to
restore the fixed count and one-component condition raises an error.

The `quality` field contains fixed class representatives for metadata. Spread
uses `habitat_class` conductance and the accepted Goal 001 water-neighbour
edge-cost equation. Edge costs and arrival times are not rescaled or
homogenized across maps.

## Descriptors and feasibility

`describe_landscape()` reports exact class counts, source-reachable fraction,
passable component count, deterministic unit-shock least-cost arrival median
and q90 at rho=1 and eta=1, geometric rook-path detour relative to the nearest
source Manhattan distance, corridor-ablation arrival ratios after replacing
corridor cells with matrix, and edge-cost heterogeneity. Geometric detours
exclude source cells; corridor ablation recomputes the actual graph edges.

All twelve map records have exact inventories, fixed source geometry, one
passable component, source-reachable fraction 1.0, finite descriptors, and
passing access/balanced sampling checks. Each sampling pair has 120 training
and 40 common holdout sites per design, with the declared stratum allocation,
cluster separation, site reachability and access-network checks.

Across the twelve design-stage maps, the observed descriptor ranges are:

| archetype | arrival median | arrival q90 | detour median | detour q90 | corridor-ablation median | corridor-ablation q90 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| connected | 51.11--78.51 | 105.28--120.35 | 1.000--1.000 | 1.058--1.159 | 1.012--1.036 | 1.078--1.094 |
| corridor | 46.25--52.59 | 92.12--123.73 | 1.000--1.000 | 1.058--1.159 | 1.187--1.555 | 1.547--2.140 |
| barrier | 95.17--107.95 | 208.85--216.20 | 1.453--1.511 | 2.139--2.179 | 1.047--1.072 | 1.088--1.110 |

These ranges are construction diagnostics. They are not prospective recovery
thresholds or a substitute for the numerical review and scientific comparison
specified in Goal 046. In particular, three map realizations per archetype
remain a bounded generality test, and the fixed road lattice and source
geometry limit transfer beyond this synthetic family.

## Review outcome and remaining boundary

The design is mechanically feasible for the stated pilot and production map
seeds, and the gallery makes the intended structural differences visible. The
maps and sampling designs still require the explicitly bounded numerical pilot
and the subsequent production decision under Goal 046. No claim about
sampling recovery, prey-effect identifiability, or biological success is made
here.
