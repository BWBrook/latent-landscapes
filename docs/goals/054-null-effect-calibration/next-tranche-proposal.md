# Proposed next tranche: Arc 4 synthesis and capstone design

30 September 2026 (Hobart). **Proposal for Fable's critique and Barry's
scientific-scope decision; no new campaign launched.** Routine compute and
timing remain delegated to Astra.

## Decision and reason

Freeze Goal 054 and use the next tranche to assemble Arc 4's paper-level
argument and a reviewable capstone protocol. I recommend no further calibration
axis or stress-generator campaign before that synthesis. This advances Charter
Pillars 4 and 5 and the transition from Roadmap Arc 4 to Arc 5.

Goals 050–054 now distinguish adequacy, prediction, estimation and effect
declaration. The original detector can miss misleading estimates; held-out
comparison carries some information but often leaves background models
ambiguous; adjustment reduces background confounding at a substantial cost
when background is constant; and the calibrated adjusted test gives a useful
conditional error/sensitivity trade-off. Goal 054's nonlinear pair did not
find a failure boundary. That negative result should remain in the paper,
without extending the stress field until a preferred example appears.

These results are sufficient to decide what a capstone should audit. They
do not complete Pillar 4 automatically, validate continuous or estimated
nuisances, or establish an effect-magnitude confidence procedure. Consolidation
should expose those remaining limits before another large computation.

## Bounded deliverables

1. **A claim-to-evidence matrix for Arc 4.** Use frozen Goals 050–054 as the
   primary evidence and Goals 046–049 for numerical and calibration boundaries.
   Map each proposed paper claim to its estimand, generator, model set,
   calibration rule, sampling unit, uncertainty, numerical qualification and
   limitation. Keep retrospective analyses, forecasts and prospective results
   distinct. Retain the unconfirmed Goal 051 nomination and all earlier
   failures. Do not pool operating rates from different rules or panels as if
   they were a common experiment.
2. **A compact draft of the Arc 4 results and discussion, with two or three
   reusable figures.** Organise it around hidden error, the cost of adjustment,
   and the distinction between a declaration and an accurate estimate.
   Report predictive ambiguity wherever the evidence does not select a model.
   Reuse saved histories, statistics and bootstrap draws. No fresh histories,
   new fitted models, new calibration or post hoc optimum cutoff are needed.
3. **One concrete capstone protocol, ending at the review gate.** Specify a
   generic ecological landscape, stochastic invasion, biased access, imperfect
   observation and prey response. Choose only complications justified by the
   earlier arcs. Define the constant-background, adjusted-background and
   appropriate neutral analyses; fix the diagnostics and the acceptable claim
   boundaries before truth is revealed. Specify what the analyst sees, what is
   sealed, who generates and audits it, and how leakage is avoided. Truth must
   actually be concealed from the analysis role if blinding is claimed.
4. **An explicit Pillar 4/5 completion assessment.** Identify which charter
   questions the existing evidence answers conditionally, which remain
   unanswered, and whether the proposed capstone can answer them. If a material
   capability is missing, specify one smallest prerequisite and its scientific
   value rather than assuming another open-ended campaign.

The capstone protocol must settle the consequential choices: whether the
invasion-alignment covariate is supplied from landscape information or estimated
from observations; which nuisances the analyst receives; whether an effect
decision is licensed by existing calibration or would require new qualification;
and which checks are performed before ecological interpretation. Existing
tables cannot silently transfer to a new landscape, survey or nuisance regime.
Any corresponding implementation, calibration or generation belongs to a
subsequent approved tranche.

## Proposed acceptance and review

Each main claim must have a traceable, reviewed evidence row. The draft must
retain both the constant-background flexibility cost and the useful adjusted
decision, the Berger–Boos negative result, the unresolved nonlinear contrast,
and the distinction between joint calibration validity and the realised table.
Figures must show comparable quantities with their correct uncertainty and
denominators. No confidence, causal or generality claim can be inferred from
descriptive prediction or likelihood geometry alone.

The capstone plan must be executable after approval: named inputs, generator
and analysis information, candidate models, planned contrasts, stopping rules,
truth-sealing/reveal procedure, existing component reuse and a measured
qualification plan. It must state whether the current scientific tools suffice.
A finding that a limited prerequisite is needed is an acceptable design
outcome; it is not authority to implement that prerequisite immediately.

Astra should obtain one independent claim-level review of this synthesis and
protocol, then give Fable the compact claim matrix, draft and protocol as a
single review package. Barry's approval would concern scientific scope and
the capstone's information/observation design. Resource and timing choices
would remain with Astra.

## Cost, stopping point and alternatives deferred

This proposed tranche reuses local reduced evidence. Plan at most 30 minutes
of computation for authenticated extraction, figure rendering and checks,
plus focused scientific drafting and one independent review. This is a planning
allowance, not a new hard resource gate. It requires no new calibration bank,
process simulations or remote production run. Goal 054's measured three-hour
production provides an anchor for later costing, but cannot price an as-yet
unspecified capstone; its full-workload qualification must do that.

Stop at the reviewed evidence synthesis and concrete capstone protocol. Do not
launch the capstone, add confidence calibration, estimate new nuisances,
change surveys, expand landscapes, commit, push or publish within this tranche.
Code consolidation can be inventoried at this arc boundary, but changes to
frozen numerical sources or removal of provenance-bearing paths require their
own bounded task.

Deferred alternatives include temporal background trends, off-grid beta,
estimated detection/spread/background level, observation redesign and broader
landscape replication. Each could matter. The proposed synthesis should rank
them against the paper's remaining claim, rather than selecting one because
compute is available. The present result provides no reason for an unchanged
rerun or additional reference replication.
