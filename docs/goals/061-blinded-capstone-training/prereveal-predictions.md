# Astra's prereveal predictions

5 October 2026, Hobart. These predictions are made after inspecting the audited
training fits and profiles, and before access to held-out observations or
generating truths. Fable's message 1024 prompted this explicit record. They
will be included in the training lock and checked at the appropriate reveals.
They are attributable scientific judgments, not additional calibrated tests.

## Basis and meaning

The public truth law assigns probability 1/2 to h=1 and otherwise draws h
log-uniformly from [2,5]. Independently, the background is exact family with
probability 3/4 and B100-A with probability 1/4. Four independent draws do not
force any particular composition. This law supplies context for the forecasts;
no posterior distribution has been calculated.

For the effect-class forecast, compare the existing adjusted profile score at
h=1 with the best stored profile score over h in [2,5]. The strength labels
describe their relative separation; they are not probabilities, confidence
levels or empirical reliability estimates. In particular, a non-declaration
does not by itself support a null-truth forecast.

For the background branch, the evidence does not identify a residual field:
both candidate background fits belong to the working family, and no branch-
specific likelihood or residual adequacy test was fitted. The exact-family
forecast below is therefore a weak, prior-modal judgment. A strong fitted
exposure gradient is compatible with both branches because B100-A is itself
exposure-aligned.

## Predictions about held-out scores

The forecast concerns the registered total conditional-prey log score over
40 held-out sites. I nominate a leading pair in each case because the relevant
saved fits are identical or nearly tied. This is a prediction of comparative
performance; all four models will still be scored and reported.

| Case | Predicted leading pair | Confidence and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| case-00 | Adjusted free-h and adjusted h=1 | Low. They are exactly the same fitted parameters and lead training by only about 0.50 nat. |
| case-01 | Adjusted free-h and adjusted h=1 | Moderate. Their parameters nearly coincide, and adjustment gains about 10.51 training nats. |
| case-02 | Constant free-h and adjusted free-h | Very low. These fits nearly coincide; their training advantage over h=1 is only about 0.05 nat. |
| case-03 | Adjusted free-h and adjusted h=1 | Very low. They nearly coincide, and adjustment gains only about 0.096 training nat. |

These predictions extrapolate the training ordering. They do not assert that
additional flexibility will generalize, and disagreement with them will be
reported directly. The effect-class forecast and the predicted held-out winner
answer different questions: a flexible fitted model could predict well even
when its effect estimate is not the generating value.

## What will count as agreement or disagreement

After holdout release, report the exact four totals and all pairwise score
differences from the frozen fits. Define the realized winner set as all models
attaining the maximum saved total. A leading-pair forecast is a hit if and only
if that set intersects the named pair; report any co-winner outside the pair
and retain exact ties as ties. Always show that each forecast named two of four candidates; do not
present a pair hit as an exact-winner prediction. Report the winning margin
so a minute score difference is visible rather than treated as decisive.

After the separate truth release, report each effect-class hit or miss and
absolute log-h error for the h=1 point forecast, alongside the error of the
unchanged continuous estimate. Report branch hits or misses, conditional beta
error for exact-family truths, and exact-grid hits plus absolute errors for mu
and detection SD. Evaluate the broad SD sets separately from the point
predictions. Do not substitute an abstention for a correct non-declaration or
reinterpret a primary forecast after the reveal.

These four case comparisons will test the recorded interpretation. They will
not be combined into a claimed power, calibration or general recovery rate.
