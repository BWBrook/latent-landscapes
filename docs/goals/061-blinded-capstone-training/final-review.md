completion on four neutral synthetic cases; all required fits and reference
tables were numerically usable; the registered rank chain produced
`p_BB = 1, 1, 0.422, 1`; and therefore the procedure **did not declare an

For that claim to hold, the returned records must be the source-bound terminal
records; the 765-node screens and 299 required effect tables must be complete;
the independent audit arrays must reconstruct the retained sets, ranks and
decisions; and the report must keep the inference within its registered scope.
That scope is casewise finite-grid false-declaration control for an exact-family,
on-grid `h=1` null, conditional on the fixed map, design, numerical bank and
algorithm. It does not include four-case family-wise control, B100-A or
off-grid validity, power, effect recovery, likelihood-profile coverage or
empirical operating-rate validation.

Plausible overturning failures were incomplete or substituted histories,
dropped numerical failures, a screen/rank mismatch, confusion of
non-declaration with abstention or proof of `h=1`, an optimizer failure large
enough to change the case-02 rank, or expansion of the conditional guarantee
beyond the registered exact-family grid.

## Strongest threats, ranked by scientific consequence

1. **Major if the claim is broadened: conditional scope.** The finite-grid
   argument does not cover B100-A, off-grid nuisance values or a different map,
   design, bank or algorithm. The four cases are not calibration replicates and
   support no family-wise, power, recovery or coverage rate. This does not
   defeat the present claim because the report states these restrictions.
2. **Major residual numerical uncertainty: global optimization.** Convergence,
   direct score reconstruction, profiles and the 1,350-point lattices provide
   strong checks, but they do not prove that every stored continuous fit is a
   global maximum. Case 02 also has broad likelihood support. The rank
   sensitivity above shows only that, holding the retained set and reference
   statistics fixed, a small change in the observed statistic is immaterial to
   the decision. It is not a bound on optimization errors in the reference
   fits; errors large enough to change the reference statistics, retention or
   the free-versus-null improvement could still alter it.
3. **Moderate provenance limitation: partial generator independence.** Effect
   scores and validity were checked exhaustively, but generator replay sampled
   three histories per node and reused the qualified spread kernel. It cannot
   alone exclude an isolated internally coherent substitution or a shared
   spread-kernel defect. Frozen-source custody, address/seed checks, row hashes
   and checkpoint constraints supply the remaining evidence.
4. **Minor interpretive risk: forecasts.** The prereveal forecasts are informed
   by these training fits and include two-of-four leading pairs. They are useful
   prospective judgments for the later reveal, but must never be presented as
   pre-training predictions, calibrated probabilities or confirmatory tests.
5. **Minor mechanical closure dependency: root manifest.** The human-readable
   lock note and all evidence it will bind are present, but the root manifest
   and its external checksum must still be constructed and verified. This does
   not weaken the scientific result; omitting or mis-hashing them would leave
   the final custody commitment incomplete.

No issue of scientific consequence arose from likelihood-support profiles
