# Goal 035: Paper 1 Experimental Design Charter

## Objective

Convert the approved programme-level discussion of 16 August 2026 into a
durable scientific charter and adaptive implementation roadmap for the
synthetic methods paper.

## Deliverables

- `docs/paper1-experimental-design-charter.md` defines the paper's central
  claim, scope, evidence pillars, definition of done, stopping rule, and
  governance;
- `docs/paper1-adaptive-implementation-roadmap.md` defines the scientific arcs,
  decision gates, layered sensitivity design, consolidation cadence, and goal
  selection rule; and
- repository orientation surfaces link to the new governing documents while
  retaining the existing detailed goal history.

## Acceptance

- the current single landscape is described as a reference laboratory rather
  than the paper's endpoint;
- process and observation sensitivity, landscape and sampling generality,
  model discrimination, and a synthetic-realistic capstone each have a clear
  scientific purpose and exit gate;
- the design avoids both an exhaustive full factorial and an uninterpretable
  all-factor space-filling experiment;
- the definition of done is ambitious but finite;
- leanification occurs at scientific arc boundaries;
- future goals must identify the paper decision or evidence surface they
  advance; and
- no simulation, inference, scientific retuning, or source-code refactor is
  performed.

## Boundary

This is a programme-design and documentation goal. It does not prescribe exact
factor ranges, sample sizes, landscape counts, generating models, or future
goal numbers. Those remain human-reviewed scientific decisions made as each arc
is entered.

No commit or push is authorised by this goal. Stop for Barry's review of the
written charter and roadmap.

## Outcome

Barry approved Goal 035 on 16 August 2026. The charter and roadmap are accepted
as the governing Paper 1 direction. They preserve an adaptive goal cadence while
requiring future work to advance a named evidence pillar, research arc,
scientific decision, or manuscript output.

No simulation, inference, source-code refactor, factor-range decision, or
future scientific goal was started. Following acceptance, the standing
publication authority for completed goals applies to this documentation
tranche.
