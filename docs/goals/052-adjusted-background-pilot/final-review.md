## Findings retained in the synthesis

- P1 has limited support: aligned-null h falls by 0.142 on average, from only
  two changing blocks. P2 is descriptively supported: baseline-threefold raw
  adjusted-null LR exceeds aligned-null LR in every paired block. Half the
  baseline-threefold LR≤2 profiles nevertheless reach h=1.
- P3 is not supported: oracle h profiles narrow in only 12/24 histories and
  their mean width changes by 0.046 h units. Oracle-minus-latent adjusted-null
  LR is positive in 13/24 and averages only 0.174.
- In aligned threefold histories, mean h changes from 3.633 to 2.750, but mean
  absolute log error worsens from 0.310 to 0.402. The direct held-out
  adjusted-minus-constant contrast is −0.334 nats on average, with three of six
  positive. Reduced average inflation is not evidence of improved recovery.
- The direct model predictive comparison is exploratory in this pilot. The
  registered adjusted-versus-adjusted-null contrast answers a different question.
  The prospective proposal now distinguishes them explicitly.
- Broad connected h–beta ridges persist in both lanes. This suggests arrival
  uncertainty alone does not account for weak separation. It does not isolate
  survey information, prey-observation noise or omitted G, or establish
  structural non-identifiability.
- Both +2 beta edge fits arise from block 0 across its paired null cells.
  They are a shared truncation pattern, not independent failures. No h=10
  maximum occurs. Small coarse/fine LR changes accompany a few coordinate
  shifts; all registered flags remain visible.

## Next-step advice incorporated

The reviewer supports the proposed fresh six-cell comparison: beta=0 and
beta=+0.60 exact controls, each at h=1 and 3, plus the original B100-A conditions.
Known-beta fits provide a cheap diagnostic of nuisance-estimation cost. Direct
held-out adjusted-minus-constant scores and paired absolute log errors should
be primary reported contrasts. A negative-beta production arm is unnecessary
for this question. One thousand paired blocks are justified by the proposed
Monte Carlo precision targets; bootstrap full blocks jointly across cells.
Limit full oracle geometry to a prespecified subset. Calibration and effect
declarations remain separate scientific work.
