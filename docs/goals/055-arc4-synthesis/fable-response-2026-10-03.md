## Manuscript interpretation

Accept the proposed question-led structure, ecological condition names and
supplementary registered-outcomes table. Keep the original negative results and
provenance, while moving internal goal/pillar language out of the paper's main
argument. The effect-set null counts belong with effect decisions, not model
adequacy. These are recommendations for the next manuscript revision, not a
claim that the current draft and figures have already been rewritten.

Fable's [information note](../../advisor/fable-information-bounds.md) supplies
an explicit complete-data rate-ratio explanation. In that limiting model,
a supplied mean 20% too low shifts log h by log(1.25), about 0.223. The larger
observed shifts in the integrated adjusted fits are consistent with that
mechanism plus nuisance trade-offs; they are not an exact identity for the
observed-data estimator or a demonstrated causal decomposition. The 32.7%
constant-rule false-declaration rate under aligned background and a low mean
is already in the verified synthesis. Terminal non-detection and the
absolute-fit explanation should remain labelled mechanistic interpretations.

## Proposed feasibility study

Recommend approval of the existing unknown-mean, extended-calendar and shared
site-detection likelihood study, with **a defensible, costed calibration route
as a required deliverable**. Preserve the 16 openly labelled development
histories, limiting-case checks and proposed four-hour first benchmark ceiling.
No large reference campaign or sealed history is included in that request.

Fable's 9 mean nodes by 5 dispersion nodes give a naive multiplier
45 × 45 = 2,025 when both reference nodes and exhaustive fitting expand.
That is a useful scaling warning, not a measured runtime forecast. Compare
profile optimization or coarse-to-fine fitting with exact reduced fixtures;
cost integration, fits and reference generation together. Optimizer accuracy,
boundaries and failure handling must be fixed and checked before calibration.

The proposed 99-reference nuisance screen followed by 999-reference effect
tests is a candidate, not an adopted exact procedure. Specify the nuisance
statistic, exclusion threshold, ties, empty-set rule, random-number allocation,
and fixed versus adaptive sample counts; then establish true-node inclusion
and Monte Carlo rank validity for that algorithm. The existing
[Goal 054 argument](../054-null-effect-calibration/registration-2026-09-29.md)
is a useful starting point, not a proof of this new procedure.

Agree that a valid finite-grid argument and an exhaustive empirical
operating-rate experiment are different requirements. Recommend costing a
reduced qualification package separately from a domain-wide evaluation, while
retaining independent numerical and null-control checks. Changing the present
protocol's mandatory domain-wide evaluation remains a proposed amendment for
Barry. **On-grid validity cannot be claimed for continuous off-grid truths or
uncovered residual-field models.** The study must resolve that mismatch before
any truth is drawn; a few successful checks cannot supply the missing bound.

Register Fable's SE(log h) range 0.40–0.60 and detection-SD recovery within
about 0.03 as forecasts with explicit summaries, not acceptance gates. Inspect
full profiles; report curvature only for resolved interior maxima. SE above
0.60 alone does not establish a calibrated test's power. Pre-invasion losses
also mean the 14 visits are not 14 known-occupied trials at every site. The
16-history panel can expose weak information and numerical cost, but cannot
validate recovery rates, confidence coverage or the proposed forecasts as
general operating characteristics.
