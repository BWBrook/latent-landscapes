# Goal 055 Arc 4 claim-to-evidence audit

2 October 2026 (Hobart). This audit consolidates frozen Goals 049--054 and the
verified Goal 055 archive checks. It
does not pool their operating rates, retrospectively alter their rules, or
complete Charter Pillar 4. The paper-level question is whether an incorrect
prey/background or observation model can yield misleading estimates, whether
that failure is detectable, and whether a protected effect declaration remains
possible. The corresponding estimands are different: adequacy rejection,
effect-estimation error, held-out score contrast, and false-declaration/
sensitivity of a named rule.

The data-generating unit throughout Goals 050--055 is one complete synthetic
history, including latent process and all observations. Most decisive contrasts
use paired histories within a replicate block; intervals therefore resample
whole blocks and preserve pairing. Inference remains conditional on one
landscape, one 120/40 training/holdout split and survey, fixed finite parameter
grids, known spread and observation nuisances except where deliberately
misspecified, and the fixed K131072/alpha1 integration bank. Held-out sites
assess within-landscape prediction; they are not independent landscape
replicates.

## Background-information audit

Fable's central concern is correct for the current declaration procedure:
Goal 054 supplied the true scalar whole-map mean background hazard, .002/day,
to both fitted models. Goal 055 deliberately substitutes .0016 and .0025/day
while leaving generating truth and observations unchanged. This is a new
sensitivity check, not an audit of an uncertain original input contract.
In Goals 052--054 the adjusted model also received the
landscape proxy A, but B100-A and N100-A retained spatial rate structure that
was not supplied exactly; residual G was omitted. The supplied map mean does
not determine training- or holdout-site means ([Goal 052 synthesis](../052-adjusted-background-pilot/synthesis.md)). Thus "known mean background hazard"
must not be shortened to "known background rate" when the latter could imply
known site-specific hazards or known generating structure.

Both checks are complete and preserve the earlier experiments. The supplied-mean
check triggers the pre-invasion design branch, and the detection check triggers
both the absolute and paired observation-model prerequisites. All 12,000 new
condition fits concern 8,000 unique original histories, with four additional
correct-mean columns reused. The two archives remain separately paired.
Correct-mean point estimates are identical to Goal 054; Goal 055 intervals use
its new registered bootstrap seed and therefore need not equal the frozen
Goal 054 intervals exactly.

The wrong-mean checks also change magnitude: adjusted h3 signed log error is
about +.26 with the low mean and -.26 to -.28 with the high mean, while mean
absolute error worsens under both. In D20 h1, adjusted large-error frequency
is 64.8% versus 30.8% in its baseline. Direct adjusted-minus-constant held-out
scores remain negative under D20, -.142 and -.093 **total nats over 40 sites**
at h1/h3. Prediction and effect declaration therefore remain distinct; divide
both a score estimate and its interval by 40 before labelling nats/site.

The safe wording is: *the Goal 054 decision procedure received the true map
mean; spatial background structure was fitted or deliberately omitted*. Its
earlier result remains conditional on that information. The new wrong-mean
checks cannot change the original eight-cell experiment or validate a random
external estimate without an explicit measurement model and new qualification.

## Decision supported now

Arc 4 conditionally establishes that detectability, protected declaration and
magnitude recovery are separate properties. It shows one selected hidden-error
construction, one detectable observation departure, conditional magnitude and
predictive gains from spatial adjustment, and a finite-grid declaration rule
with useful sensitivity under its stated nuisance assumptions. Goal 055 locates
two failures when those assumptions change. The selected capstone design now
requires pre-invasion information and a persistent site-detection model, each
still subject to observed-data feasibility and new calibration. It does not yet satisfy the Charter requirement to
assess each dataset under a prespecified competing-model set, because the
screen, later adjustment experiments and calibrated decision are linked but
not one common blinded comparison. The original Goal 051 nominee is still
unconfirmed. Pillar 4 therefore has substantial conditional evidence but is
not complete; Pillar 5 remains untested pending a separately reviewed blinded
capstone.
